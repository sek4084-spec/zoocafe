# ZOO:CAFE v31.2 — Living Cafe prototype

Based on v31.1 large-background cafe direction.

Changes:
- Restored the original v31 center rug/table art direction and extended the cut lower area.
- Cafe collision remains code-driven; the rug itself is walkable and furniture is solid.
- Safer cafe spawn and slightly smaller indoor collision box to reduce getting stuck.
- Added subtle ambient animation layers over the static cafe art:
  - pendant light glow pulse
  - moving window highlights/leaves
  - intermittent espresso steam
  - sleeping-cat breathing/tail accent
  - very subtle room/plant shimmer
- Cafe keeper now has a tiny breathing/bobbing idle and occasional blink.
- NPC asset remains replaceable at images/npc/cafe-owner.png.

The main cafe art is still assets/cafe/interior/cafe-background.png so future visual edits can be made one step at a time.
